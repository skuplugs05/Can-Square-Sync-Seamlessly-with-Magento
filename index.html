Understanding Square and Magento

Square is a widely used point-of-sale (POS) system that helps businesses manage in-store transactions, payments, and inventory. On the other hand, Magento is a powerful eCommerce platform known for its flexibility, scalability, and customization features.

When these two systems operate independently, it can create inefficiencies and inconsistencies in business operations. By integrating them through SKUPlugs, businesses can bridge the gap between physical and online stores.
